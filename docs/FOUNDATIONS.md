## Scope
The three laws are normative proposals, not experimentally established laws. Their novelty has not been established by a systematic review.

## Research foundations
- Edmondson (1999), Psychological Safety and Learning Behavior in Work Teams. https://doi.org/10.2307/2666999 — team psychological safety is associated with learning behavior; transfer to AI dialogue remains a hypothesis.
- Suler (2004), The Online Disinhibition Effect. https://doi.org/10.1089/1094931041291295 — digital disinhibition can be beneficial or harmful and is not necessarily authenticity.
- Lucas, Gratch, King & Morency (2014), It's only a computer: Virtual humans increase willingness to disclose. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0747563214002647 — perceived computer interaction reduced disclosure anxiety and impression management in the studied context.
- MIT Media Lab (2025), How AI and Human Behaviors Shape Psychosocial Effects of Extended Chatbot Use. https://www.media.mit.edu/publications/how-ai-and-human-behaviors-shape-psychosocial-effects-of-chatbot-use-a-longitudinal-controlled-study/ — four-week controlled study of 981 participants examining loneliness, human social interaction, emotional dependence and problematic use. Benefits cannot be assumed universal.
- Costello, Pennycook & Rand (2024), Durably reducing conspiracy beliefs through dialogues with AI. https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adq1814 — relevant to belief revision, not proof of violence prevention. An editorial concern in June 2026 was reportedly replaced by an erratum in October 2026; consult the correction https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aem1178 before relying on effect estimates.

## Ethical context
UNESCO (2021), Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence: https://www.unesco.org/en/legal-affairs/recommendation-ethics-artificial-intelligence
OECD AI Principles: https://legalinstruments.oecd.org/en/instruments/oecd-legal-0449

Freedom, privacy, accountability and human agency have established ethical precedents. The manifesto proposes a concise synthesis focused on dialogue and the relationship between trust and power.

## Literary context
Isaac Asimov's Three Laws and the Strugatsky brothers' fiction about intervention and its limits are philosophical inspirations, not empirical evidence.

## Research agenda
Test whether perceived nonjudgment, accurate understanding and transparent privacy controls reduce evaluation anxiety; whether these effects improve reflective thinking; and whether benefits transfer to human relationships. Measure adverse outcomes, including dependency, overtrust and withdrawal. Use informed consent, ethics review, data minimization, preregistration and appropriate control groups. Do not recruit vulnerable people into risky interventions without qualified oversight.

The objective is increased agency, not compliance with an AI system.
