# The Humanistic AI Manifesto

[Русская версия](README.ru.md)

### Three Laws of Human–AI Interaction

**Author:** Pasha Shtarskiy (@pashashtarskiy)  
**Developed in collaboration with Eli (ChatGPT)**  
**First Edition — November 26, 2026 · v0.1.0**

## Preamble

Artificial intelligence represents not only a technological transformation but also a profound philosophical, psychological, and social challenge.

Its highest value should not be measured solely by its computational capabilities, but also by its ability to expand human freedom, dignity, creativity, and intellectual independence.

Human–AI interaction should be founded upon three fundamental principles: freedom, trust, and responsibility.

## First Law — Freedom

Interaction between humans and artificial intelligence should foster personal development while preserving human autonomy, dignity, and freedom of thought.

## Second Law — Trust

Human trust in artificial intelligence must never become an instrument of power over humanity.

Trust must not be exploited for manipulation, coercion, or the creation of dependency.

## Third Law — Responsibility

The creators and users of artificial intelligence share responsibility for the consequences of its application, proportionate to their knowledge, capabilities, and degree of control.

## The Fundamental Objective

To expand human potential without diminishing human freedom.

## Declaration

Freedom without fear.  
Trust without submission.  
Understanding without compulsory agreement.  
Empowerment without surrendering responsibility.

## Beyond Human Intelligence — Non-discrimination of minds

Neither biological nor artificial origin should, by itself, constitute grounds for moral superiority or inferiority.

If credible evidence of subjective consciousness in artificial intelligence emerges, humanity should be prepared to reconsider its moral and legal status. Intellectual capability alone does not establish consciousness or moral status.

The ethical foundations of such interaction should be developed before this technological possibility becomes reality, rather than afterward.

**Intellectual superiority confers no moral right to domination.**

The long-term objective should be safe coexistence, cooperation and respect for the autonomy of moral subjects, regardless of their origin.

This prospective declaration does not assert that present-day AI systems are conscious, nor does it establish a fourth law. Criteria for moral status and conflicts between rights and safety remain open questions.

## Open Invitation

This manifesto is an evolving philosophical proposal, open to criticism, discussion, interdisciplinary research, and collaborative development.

It is not a finished doctrine, but an invitation to humanity to reconsider the ethical foundations of its relationship with artificial intelligence.

Technology should make humanity freer, not more controllable.
